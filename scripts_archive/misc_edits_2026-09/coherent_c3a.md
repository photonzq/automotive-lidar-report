**Two coherent specialists up close: Analog Photonics and Pointcloud** (public records unless tagged)

*Analog Photonics* (Boston; founded about 2012 by Michael Watts of MIT, who remains CEO [P/S]):
- **Public money, from USAspending:** **\$45.2 M** of federal prime obligations over 54 transactions (UEI EA5NMDFQBNF4) [I]. The largest awards:
  - DARPA MOABB/"ARGOS", **\$26.6 M**, 2016–2023, full and open competition [I];
  - DARPA "Optical Phased Arrays at Scale" (OPAS), **\$6.36 M**, 2021–24, sole source, to "manufacture OPAs and integrated lidar demonstrators at a domestic advanced photonics facility" [I];
  - ONR, "Long range lidar and free space optical com transmitter based on SiPh optical phased arrays", **\$3.97 M**, 2023–26 [I];
  - an ONR free-space optical communications (FSOC) transceiver contract (2026) [I, not individually checked].
- **SBIR/STTR is only ≈\$4.3 M of it** (10 awards, 2015–2026) [I, SBIR.gov]. The company does not live on SBIRs; it lives on large DARPA and ONR contracts.
- **Subawards:** reported subawards (AIM Photonics PDK work, NCMS, Draper and others) add roughly \$34 M more. Some may be double-counted amendments, and they were not checked individually [I/S].
- **Private money:** no venture round found. Aggregators list agencies and MassVentures as "investors" [S].
- **Size:** 26 employees (SBIR.gov) [I].
- **Automotive:** the company claims joint development agreements with two unnamed Tier 1s (2018, 2020). Its public milestones stop in 2021 [P]. The FS-4S datasheet (2025) is analysed in §C10.2b.
- **Direction:**
  - defence FSOC;
  - integrated lasers and isolators (Army SBIR, 2026);
  - two NASA SBIRs (Sep 2026);
  - a DARPA DWDM transceiver SBIR;
  - AIM Photonics PDK services [I].
- **Key-person risk:** the lead author of its OPA papers, Christopher Poulton, now lists a co-founder/CTO role at Beacon Photonics [S, LinkedIn].
- **Reading** [E]: a contract R&D house funded by US defence photonics. Lidar is one application alongside FSOC and lasers, and no evidence points to an automotive volume business.

*Pointcloud* (Pointcloud Inc., San Francisco, incorporated Q1 2017; Pointcloud GmbH, Zürich, registered Mar 2022, now headquarters and main R&D; Pointcloud Europe Ltd, UK, 2017) [P/I]:
- **People:** CEO Remus Nicolaescu; roughly 10–20 staff [S].
- **Private money:** ≈\$4.3 M disclosed by aggregators, from investors including Heuristic Capital Partners, Propel(x), Taihill Venture, Plug and Play and Baidu Ventures [S].
- **Public money: none found.** There are no US federal awards (USAspending) and no CORDIS or EIC project [I]. Swiss firms were shut out of the EIC Accelerator in 2021–24 [S]; Innosuisse was not checked.
- **Roadmap** (company deck, Munich, Jun 2025) [P]:
  - Q2 2020: 16 × 16 monostatic and 32 × 16 bistatic demonstrators;
  - Q1 2021: the bistatic *Nature* paper;
  - Q1 2022: "First major Tier 1 commercial contract win", and the GmbH incorporated;
  - Q1 2025: engineering samples (352 × 176 and 192 × 64);
  - Q2 2026: next-generation samples, "short, mid & long range".
  - An earlier (2024) deck had the 2025 samples at Q3 2024 [S].
- **Technical record:**
  - *Nature* 2026: all authors at Pointcloud GmbH; no public funding acknowledged [I].
  - A development kit with FPGA processing for 48 channels [P].
  - 11 patent families [S].
- **Reading** [E]:
  - A lean, venture-funded chip developer with a claimed Tier 1 contract.
  - Its coherent FPA, with in-pixel TIAs, is the closest public step toward the "coherent SPAD-SoC" FMCW would need (§C7b).
  - Its stated next step, an ASIC to replace RFSoC readout, is the channel-tax problem itself.
